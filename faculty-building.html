The Faculty of Computing and Informatics Building

This is where lecturers, department offices, and staff you'll meet throughout your programme are based.

Finding your way around

The faculty building currently consists of two buildings.The first one is a two storey building. It houses the Dean's office, Computer Science HOD's office, Information Technology(IFT)/Library and Information Science(LIS) HOD's office, the faculty officer's office, and other faculty personnel.

The second building is a bungalow behind computer lab two(2). It houses the HOD's office, Cyber Security(CYB)/Software Engineering(SWE)

If you get lost on your first day, look out for departmental noticeboards or look for any nearest HOD'S office for respective direction. Take note, ensure to be well dressed and be organized.

The faculty building (building 1) is located opposite the school physics lab take off site, while the second building (2) is located behind computer lab 2

Inside the first building(storey)

  • Dean's office and Faculty administration
  • Deans Personal Assistant(PA)
  • HOD's office(Computer Science and IFT/LIS)
  • HOD's personal Assistant(PA)
  • Staff common room

Inside the second building

  • HOD's office(Cyber Security/ Software Engineering)
  • HOD's personal Assistant(PA)
  • Staff common room
