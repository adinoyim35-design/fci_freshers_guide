Library and Information Science

Organising, preserving and providing access to knowledge and information resources.

OverviewComputer ScienceCyber SecuritySoftware EngineeringInformation TechnologyLibrary & Info Science

What the department focuses on

  • Information organisation and classification
  • Digital libraries and repositories
  • Records and archives management
  • Information retrieval systems
  • Research methods and information literacy

Where graduates work

  • Librarian / archivist
  • Information/knowledge manager
  • Digital repository administrator
  • Records manager

What to expect in Year One

  • Introduction to library and information science
  • Information sources and services
  • Introduction to computing for information professionals
  • Communication and study skills
