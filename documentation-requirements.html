Documentation Requirements

Based on CUSTECH's published admission process. Always confirm the exact list and deadlines for your session with the Registry and your department.

Before you arrive on campus

These steps generally happen online, before physical registration.

  1. Confirm your admission status on the JAMB CAPS portal, and ensure Confluence University of Science and Technology, Osara is your accepted institution.
  2. Complete the university's Pre-Admission Screening Exercise (Post-UTME / UPASE) where required, within the published deadline.
  3. Ensure your O'Level result and UTME result slip are correctly uploaded and match your JAMB profile.
  4. Pay the acceptance fee once your admission is confirmed, and keep the payment receipt.
  5. Print your admission letter from the official CUSTECH portal.

Documents to bring for physical registration

Carry original copies plus several photocopies of each document — departments often request more than one set.

DocumentNotes
JAMB admission letterPrinted from the JAMB or CUSTECH portal
JAMB result slip / UTME statement of resultOriginal and photocopies
O'Level result(s)WAEC / NECO / NABTEB, minimum of five credits including English Language and Mathematics
Birth certificate or age declarationOriginal and photocopy
Local Government Identification letterObtained from your LGA of origin
Passport photographsRecent, plain background; carry more than the minimum requested
Medical certificate of fitnessFrom a recognised hospital, as directed by the university health centre
Acceptance fee payment receiptKeep both the printed receipt and a photo/scan copy
School fees payment receiptRequired before course registration is completed

After arriving in the Faculty

Once your central university registration is done, complete these Faculty and department-level steps.

  1. Report to the Faculty of Computing and Informatics office with your registration documents for verification.
  2. Proceed to your specific department (Computer Science, Cyber Security, Software Engineering, Information Technology, or Library & Information Science) to open a student file.
  3. Meet your Level 100 Adviser to confirm your course registration for the session.
  4. Collect your departmental course reference and lab schedule from your course representative.
  5. Register on the university's e-portal for course allocation, where applicable.

Keep copies of everything

Scan or photograph every document before submitting originals. You will likely be asked for the same documents again during subsequent registrations, hostel allocation, or when applying for an ID card.
